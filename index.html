Lessons Learned

Emulators lie — real devices expose battery-saver mode, OEM overlays, and network interruptions that emulators mask. Never skip exploratory testing after automation passes: automation finds known bugs, exploration finds unknown ones. A defect found in dev costs 10× less than one found in production.
